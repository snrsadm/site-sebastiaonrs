How the books connect

Two stages of the same demand: turning decisions into results

Both books start from the same observation: most organizations don't fail for lack of vision, but because of the distance between what was decided and what the company's structure can actually sustain. One is about preparing that structure for what's coming. The other, about keeping it working after the decision has already been made.

Before the decision

Leading Organizations into the Age of Artificial Intelligence

About the architecture an organization needs (in leadership, culture, processes and data) so that artificial intelligence expands real capabilities, not just expectations.

"Artificial intelligence expands capabilities. Organizational Orchestration turns them into results."

After the decision

After December

About what sustains a strategic plan after it's approved: the mechanisms that drain it throughout the year, and the governance architecture that keeps it alive and aligned with reality through delivery.

"Strategic planning is not a document. It's a management system."

Read together, the two books cover the full cycle: from decision to delivery, from intent to results that last.
