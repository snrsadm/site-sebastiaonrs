Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, MBA in Organizational Excellence Management, Master's in Strategic Administration, and board director certification

What drives me is a simple conviction: every organization needs an architecture (in leadership, culture, processes and data) to turn decisions into results. That applies both to preparing a company for what's coming and to sustaining execution of what has already been decided. That conviction is behind everything I share here: the free diagnostics, the two books I've written, and the reflections I publish.

I brought these ideas together in Leading Organizations into the Age of Artificial Intelligence and in After December, my most recent book, both available as a free download. I keep this space for two reasons: to exchange ideas and experience with other leaders, and to contribute what I've learned along the way.
